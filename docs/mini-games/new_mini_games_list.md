# New Mini-Games List for Truth or Dare Deluxe

This document contains 80 new mini-games to be added to the existing 20 mini-games in the Drink Mode feature.

## Easy Difficulty (27 new games)

1. **Drink If** - The leader makes "Drink if..." statements. Anyone who fits the description drinks.
   
2. **Rhyme Master** - First player says a word, next player must say a word that rhymes. Continue until someone can't think of a rhyme.
   
3. **Sentence Building** - Each player adds one word to build a sentence. If the sentence becomes nonsensical, the player drinks.
   
4. **Drink Race** - On the count of three, everyone drinks their beverage. Last to finish drinks again.
   
5. **Spelling Bee** - Players take turns spelling increasingly difficult words. Misspell and drink.
   
6. **Drink Pong Solo** - Set up cups and take turns trying to land ping pong balls. Miss and drink.
   
7. **Drink Dice** - Roll a die. Drink the number shown. Roll a six for a free pass.
   
8. **Drink or Dare** - Choose to either drink or perform a dare chosen by the group.
   
9. **Thumb War** - Play thumb war with another player. Loser drinks.
   
10. **Drink Roulette** - Place cups in a circle with one containing a stronger drink. Spin and drink what's in front of you.
    
11. **Drink Limbo** - Set up a limbo bar. If you can't go under without touching, drink.
    
12. **Drink Charades** - Act out a drinking-related word or phrase. If no one guesses correctly, you drink.
    
13. **Drink Pictionary** - Draw a drinking-related word or phrase. If no one guesses correctly, you drink.
    
14. **Drink Simon Says** - Play Simon Says with drinking penalties for mistakes.
    
15. **Drink Memory** - Place cards face down. Match pairs or drink.
    
16. **Drink Hot Potato** - Pass an object around while music plays. When it stops, holder drinks.
    
17. **Drink Telephone** - Whisper a message around the circle. If the final message is wrong, everyone drinks.
    
18. **Drink Trivia** - Answer trivia questions. Wrong answers drink.
    
19. **Drink Karaoke** - Sing a portion of a song. Group votes if it's good enough or drink.
    
20. **Drink Riddles** - Solve riddles. If wrong, drink.
    
21. **Drink Rock Paper Scissors** - Play rock paper scissors. Loser drinks.
    
22. **Drink Jokes** - Tell a joke. If no one laughs, you drink.
    
23. **Drink Categories** - Name items in a category. Repeat or hesitate and drink.
    
24. **Drink Opposites** - Leader says a word, you must say its opposite. Wrong answer drinks.
    
25. **Drink Directions** - Leader says "left" or "right" and points. If they don't match, drink if you follow the pointing instead of the words.
    
26. **Drink Rhythm** - Maintain a clapping rhythm. Miss the beat and drink.
    
27. **Drink Balance** - Balance an object on your head or finger. Drop it and drink.

## Medium Difficulty (27 new games)

1. **Drink Poker** - Play a round of poker. Lowest hand drinks.
   
2. **Drink Blackjack** - Play a round of blackjack. Losers drink.
   
3. **Drink War** - Play the card game War. Loser of each battle drinks.
   
4. **Drink Quarters** - Bounce quarters into cups. Miss and drink.
   
5. **Drink Flip Cup Relay** - Teams race to flip cups. Losing team drinks.
   
6. **Drink Categories Advanced** - Name items in increasingly specific categories. Fail and drink.
   
7. **Drink Word Association Chain** - Say a word related to the previous word. Break the chain and drink.
   
8. **Drink Tongue Twisters** - Say increasingly difficult tongue twisters. Mess up and drink.
   
9. **Drink Counting** - Count from 1, replacing multiples of 7 and numbers containing 7 with "drink". Mess up and drink.
   
10. **Drink Spelling Backwards** - Spell words backwards. Fail and drink.
    
11. **Drink Blindfolded Taste Test** - Identify drinks while blindfolded. Wrong guess means drink.
    
12. **Drink Pyramid** - Draw cards and drink based on a pyramid structure.
    
13. **Drink Battleship** - Play a simplified version of Battleship. When your ship is hit, drink.
    
14. **Drink Taboo** - Describe a word without using certain related words. Fail and drink.
    
15. **Drink Jenga** - Play Jenga with drinking rules on blocks.
    
16. **Drink Dominoes** - Play dominoes. When you can't place a domino, drink.
    
17. **Drink Darts** - Throw darts at a board. Miss the board and drink.
    
18. **Drink Dice Poker** - Roll five dice. Lowest poker hand drinks.
    
19. **Drink Yahtzee** - Roll dice for Yahtzee combinations. Lowest score drinks.
    
20. **Drink Scattergories** - Write words starting with a specific letter in different categories. Duplicates drink.
    
21. **Drink Hangman** - Play Hangman. If the word is guessed, the word creator drinks. If not, the guessers drink.
    
22. **Drink Tic Tac Toe Tournament** - Play Tic Tac Toe. Losers drink.
    
23. **Drink Connect Four** - Play Connect Four. Loser drinks.
    
24. **Drink Checkers** - Play a quick game of checkers. Loser drinks.
    
25. **Drink Chess** - Play speed chess with a timer. Loser drinks.
    
26. **Drink Backgammon** - Play a simplified version of backgammon. Loser drinks.
    
27. **Drink Uno** - Play a round of Uno. Loser drinks based on cards left in hand.

## Hard Difficulty (26 new games)

1. **Drink Kings Cup** - Play Kings Cup with complex rules and drinking penalties.
   
2. **Drink Beer Pong Tournament** - Play a tournament-style beer pong with elimination rounds.
   
3. **Drink Flip Cup Championship** - Multi-round flip cup tournament with increasing difficulty.
   
4. **Drink Quarters Master** - Bounce quarters into increasingly smaller targets. Miss and drink.
   
5. **Drink Trivia Gauntlet** - Answer progressively harder trivia questions. Wrong answers mean more drinks.
   
6. **Drink Shot Roulette** - Spin to determine who takes a shot from a selection of mixed drinks.
   
7. **Drink Power Hour** - Take a small drink every minute for an hour (simulated in-game).
   
8. **Drink Stack Attack** - Stack cups in a specific pattern while timed. Fail and drink.
   
9. **Drink Speed Bartender** - Name as many cocktails as possible in 30 seconds. Lowest count drinks.
   
10. **Drink Pong Variations** - Beer pong with special rules like behind-the-back shots, non-dominant hand, etc.
    
11. **Drink Obstacle Course** - Complete a mini obstacle course while holding a drink. Spill and drink double.
    
12. **Drink Card House** - Build a house of cards. If it falls, drink based on how many cards were used.
    
13. **Drink Dice Tower** - Stack dice on top of each other. If they fall, drink based on the sum.
    
14. **Drink Blind Bartender** - Mix a cocktail blindfolded. Others rate it. Low scores drink.
    
15. **Drink Bottle Flip Challenge** - Flip bottles to land upright. Miss and drink.
    
16. **Drink Coin Stack** - Stack coins on edge. If they fall, drink based on how many were stacked.
    
17. **Drink Shot Ski** - Multiple players drink from attached shot glasses simultaneously. Last to finish drinks again.
    
18. **Drink Chandelier** - Complex cup arrangement where landing a ball in the center means everyone drinks.
    
19. **Drink Boat Race** - Teams race to finish a line of drinks. Losing team drinks an extra penalty.
    
20. **Drink Rage Cage** - Fast-paced game with multiple cups, dice, and drinking penalties.
    
21. **Drink Civil War** - Team-based beer pong with multiple cups and complex rules.
    
22. **Drink Anchorman** - Last person to put their thumb on the table when the leader does drinks.
    
23. **Drink Wizard Staff** - Stack empty cans/bottles to create a "staff". Shortest staff drinks.
    
24. **Drink Centurion** - Take a shot of beer every minute for 100 minutes (simulated in-game).
    
25. **Drink Battleshots** - Battleship game where hits mean drinks.
    
26. **Drink Thumper** - Complex rhythm game with personalized gestures. Miss your cue and drink.
