# Mini-Games Detailed Descriptions and Instructions

This document provides detailed descriptions and additional instructions for the 80 new mini-games to be added to the Truth or Dare Deluxe app.

## Easy Difficulty

### 1. Drink If
**Description:** The leader makes "Drink if..." statements. Anyone who fits the description drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player takes turns being the leader
- Statements should be about experiences or characteristics (e.g., "Drink if you've ever been to Paris")
- No targeting specific players with statements
- Leaders can't repeat statements that have already been used
- After 10 rounds, the player who drank the most becomes the new leader

### 2. Rhyme Master
**Description:** First player says a word, next player must say a word that rhymes. Continue until someone can't think of a rhyme.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Set a 5-second time limit for each player to respond
- No repeating words that have already been used
- If a player challenges the rhyme, group votes if it counts
- After a player loses, they drink and pick a new starting word
- Made-up words are not allowed unless they can be defined

### 3. Sentence Building
**Description:** Each player adds one word to build a sentence. If the sentence becomes nonsensical, the player drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- The sentence must remain grammatically correct
- After 10 words, the sentence must end with proper punctuation
- The group votes if a sentence is nonsensical
- After completing a sentence, the next player starts a new one
- Players can't add words that make the sentence impossible to continue

### 4. Drink Race
**Description:** On the count of three, everyone drinks their beverage. Last to finish drinks again.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Drinks should be approximately the same amount for fairness
- No interfering with other players
- The winner chooses the next drinking amount (within reason)
- Players must completely finish their drinks
- Spilling results in automatic last place

### 5. Spelling Bee
**Description:** Players take turns spelling increasingly difficult words. Misspell and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a word list app or dictionary for reference
- Each round increases in difficulty
- Players have 10 seconds to spell the word
- No writing down the word or using devices
- The player who spelled correctly chooses the next word

### 6. Drink Pong Solo
**Description:** Set up cups and take turns trying to land ping pong balls. Miss and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use 3-6 cups in a triangle formation
- Each player gets 2 attempts per turn
- If you make a shot, choose someone else to drink
- After 3 rounds, increase the distance to the cups
- Players can call bank shots for bonus points (opponent drinks twice)

### 7. Drink Dice
**Description:** Roll a die. Drink the number shown. Roll a six for a free pass.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Players take turns rolling the die
- The number rolled equals the number of sips to take
- Rolling a 6 means you can assign the drinks to another player
- Rolling the same number twice in a row means double drinks
- After everyone has rolled twice, highest total can give out 3 drinks

### 8. Drink or Dare
**Description:** Choose to either drink or perform a dare chosen by the group.

**Additional Instructions:**
- The player to your left presents the choice: drink or dare
- If you choose dare, the entire group must agree on the dare
- Dares should be reasonable and safe
- If you refuse the dare, you must drink double
- After 3 rounds, players who chose to drink every time must do a dare

### 9. Thumb War
**Description:** Play thumb war with another player. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Best 2 out of 3 wins
- No using your other hand to interfere
- Winners challenge new opponents
- After 3 consecutive wins, the champion can give out 3 drinks
- If there's a tie after 30 seconds, both players drink

### 10. Drink Roulette
**Description:** Place cups in a circle with one containing a stronger drink. Spin and drink what's in front of you.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use 6-8 cups in a circle
- One cup contains a stronger drink or different beverage
- Players close their eyes during setup
- After spinning, players must drink the cup in front of them
- The player who gets the special cup chooses the next special drink

### 11. Drink Limbo
**Description:** Set up a limbo bar. If you can't go under without touching, drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a broomstick or similar object as the bar
- Lower the bar after everyone successfully completes a round
- No bending knees more than 90 degrees
- Last player remaining chooses who drinks next round
- If everyone fails a round, everyone drinks

### 12. Drink Charades
**Description:** Act out a drinking-related word or phrase. If no one guesses correctly, you drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- 60-second time limit for guessing
- No speaking or sound effects
- No pointing at objects in the room
- Correct guesser gets to choose who acts next
- After 3 rounds, increase difficulty with more complex phrases

### 13. Drink Pictionary
**Description:** Draw a drinking-related word or phrase. If no one guesses correctly, you drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- 60-second time limit for drawing and guessing
- No letters, numbers, or symbols
- No speaking while drawing
- Correct guesser gets to choose who draws next
- After 3 rounds, players must draw with non-dominant hand

### 14. Drink Simon Says
**Description:** Play Simon Says with drinking penalties for mistakes.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player is Simon and gives commands
- If you follow a command not preceded by "Simon says," drink
- If you don't follow a command preceded by "Simon says," drink
- After 3 mistakes, player becomes the new Simon
- Simon drinks if everyone follows the rules correctly for 5 commands

### 15. Drink Memory
**Description:** Place cards face down. Match pairs or drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use 10-20 cards (5-10 pairs)
- Players take turns flipping two cards
- If cards match, remove them and go again
- If cards don't match, flip them back and drink
- Player with most pairs at the end gives out drinks equal to their pairs

### 16. Drink Hot Potato
**Description:** Pass an object around while music plays. When it stops, holder drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a random music player with stop/pause function
- Pass the object quickly but carefully
- No throwing or dropping the object (penalty drink)
- After 3 rounds, increase by having the holder give out a drink too
- Player controlling the music can't watch the circle

### 17. Drink Telephone
**Description:** Whisper a message around the circle. If the final message is wrong, everyone drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- First player creates a message at least 10 words long
- Each player whispers it once to the next player
- Last player says the message out loud
- If correct, first player drinks; if wrong, everyone else drinks
- Increase difficulty with longer messages each round

### 18. Drink Trivia
**Description:** Answer trivia questions. Wrong answers drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use trivia apps or cards if available
- Each player gets one question per round
- 15 seconds to answer each question
- Correct answers can give out a drink
- After 3 rounds, switch to harder questions

### 19. Drink Karaoke
**Description:** Sing a portion of a song. Group votes if it's good enough or drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a music app to play 30 seconds of a song
- Player must continue singing for at least 15 seconds after music stops
- Group votes thumbs up or down
- Majority thumbs down means drink
- Players can challenge others to specific songs

### 20. Drink Riddles
**Description:** Solve riddles. If wrong, drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use riddle apps or cards if available
- 30 seconds to solve each riddle
- One hint allowed (costs a sip)
- Correct solver gives the next riddle
- After 3 rounds, increase riddle difficulty

### 21. Drink Rock Paper Scissors
**Description:** Play rock paper scissors. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Best 2 out of 3 wins
- Winner challenges a new player
- After 3 consecutive wins, champion can give out 3 drinks
- Ties mean both players drink
- After 5 rounds, add "lizard" and "Spock" options for complexity

### 22. Drink Jokes
**Description:** Tell a joke. If no one laughs, you drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Jokes must be original or not told yet in the game
- Group votes if the joke was funny
- Majority vote determines if player drinks
- No offensive jokes allowed (automatic drink)
- After everyone has told a joke, switch to themed jokes

### 23. Drink Categories
**Description:** Name items in a category. Repeat or hesitate and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- First player chooses a category
- 5-second time limit per player
- No repeating items already mentioned
- If you hesitate more than 5 seconds, drink
- After a player loses, they choose the next category

### 24. Drink Opposites
**Description:** Leader says a word, you must say its opposite. Wrong answer drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Leader points to random players
- 3-second time limit to respond
- If there's a dispute, group votes on correctness
- After 10 words, new leader is selected
- Increase difficulty with more abstract concepts

### 25. Drink Directions
**Description:** Leader says "left" or "right" and points. If they don't match, drink if you follow the pointing instead of the words.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Leader points and says directions rapidly
- Players must follow the spoken direction, not the pointing
- If you move in the wrong direction, drink
- Increase speed as the game progresses
- After 10 commands, new leader is selected

### 26. Drink Rhythm
**Description:** Maintain a clapping rhythm. Miss the beat and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with a simple rhythm
- Each player adds one beat to the sequence
- If you miss the rhythm, drink and start over
- Increase tempo after each successful round
- After 5 rounds, add stomping or snapping

### 27. Drink Balance
**Description:** Balance an object on your head or finger. Drop it and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with 30 seconds of balancing
- Increase time by 15 seconds each round
- No touching the object once balancing begins
- Moving around makes it more challenging
- Last player to successfully balance chooses the next object

## Medium Difficulty

### 1. Drink Poker
**Description:** Play a round of poker. Lowest hand drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a simplified 5-card draw poker
- Deal 5 cards to each player
- One round of discarding and drawing
- Lowest hand drinks equal to the difference between their hand and the winning hand
- After 3 rounds, winner can create a special rule

### 2. Drink Blackjack
**Description:** Play a round of blackjack. Losers drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player acts as dealer
- Standard blackjack rules apply
- Players who bust drink twice
- Players who lose to dealer drink once
- Dealer drinks for each player who beats them
- Blackjack (21 with 2 cards) allows player to give out 3 drinks

### 3. Drink War
**Description:** Play the card game War. Loser of each battle drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Deal equal cards to two players
- Players flip top card simultaneously
- Higher card wins; loser drinks
- If cards match, "war" occurs with 3 face-down cards and 1 face-up
- Play until one player has all cards or 10 rounds

### 4. Drink Quarters
**Description:** Bounce quarters into cups. Miss and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Place cup 1-2 feet away
- Each player gets 2 attempts per turn
- Make it and assign a drink to someone else
- Miss both and drink yourself
- After everyone has gone, increase distance
- First to make 3 shots wins and creates a rule

### 5. Drink Flip Cup Relay
**Description:** Teams race to flip cups. Losing team drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Divide into equal teams
- Line up along a table
- Each player drinks and then flips their cup
- Next player can't start until previous cup is flipped
- Losing team all drinks an extra penalty
- Best 2 out of 3 rounds

### 6. Drink Categories Advanced
**Description:** Name items in increasingly specific categories. Fail and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with broad category (e.g., "Animals")
- After each round, narrow the category (e.g., "Mammals" → "Felines" → "Wild cats")
- 5-second time limit per player
- No repeating items
- Last player standing chooses next category

### 7. Drink Word Association Chain
**Description:** Say a word related to the previous word. Break the chain and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- First player says any word
- Next player must say a related word within 3 seconds
- No repeating words
- Group judges if words are related
- If chain breaks, player drinks and starts new chain
- After 10 successful chains, increase speed

### 8. Drink Tongue Twisters
**Description:** Say increasingly difficult tongue twisters. Mess up and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with simple tongue twisters
- Each round increases in difficulty
- Must say tongue twister clearly and correctly
- Three attempts allowed
- After failing, player creates a tongue twister for next player

### 9. Drink Counting
**Description:** Count from 1, replacing multiples of 7 and numbers containing 7 with "drink". Mess up and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Go around the circle counting up
- Replace multiples of 7 (7, 14, 21...) with "drink"
- Replace numbers containing 7 (7, 17, 27...) with "drink"
- If number is both, say "drink drink"
- Hesitation or mistake means drink and restart

### 10. Drink Spelling Backwards
**Description:** Spell words backwards. Fail and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with 4-letter words
- Increase length each round
- 10-second time limit
- No writing down or visualizing with fingers
- After 5 rounds, use compound words for extra challenge

### 11. Drink Blindfolded Taste Test
**Description:** Identify drinks while blindfolded. Wrong guess means drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player prepares 3-5 different drinks
- Blindfold each player for tasting
- One small sip to identify the drink
- Wrong guess means finish the sample
- Points for correct guesses; lowest score drinks extra

### 12. Drink Pyramid
**Description:** Draw cards and drink based on a pyramid structure.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Build a pyramid of face-down cards (e.g., 5-4-3-2-1)
- Players take turns drawing from remaining deck
- If drawn card matches pyramid row, assign that many drinks
- Bottom row = 1 drink, top row = 5 drinks
- After pyramid is complete, rebuild with new deck

### 13. Drink Battleship
**Description:** Play a simplified version of Battleship. When your ship is hit, drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a 5x5 grid
- Each player places 3 ships (sizes: 3, 2, 1)
- Take turns guessing coordinates
- Hit means opponent drinks
- Sink entire ship means finish your drink
- First to sink all opponent's ships wins

### 14. Drink Taboo
**Description:** Describe a word without using certain related words. Fail and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player draws a word card with 3-5 taboo words
- 60 seconds to get teammates to guess the word
- Can't say any part of the main word or taboo words
- If you say a taboo word, drink and draw new card
- Team with most correct guesses wins; other team drinks

### 15. Drink Jenga
**Description:** Play Jenga with drinking rules on blocks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Write drinking rules on Jenga blocks
- Pull blocks according to standard Jenga rules
- Follow the rule written on the pulled block
- If tower falls, player drinks 3 times and rebuilds
- After successful pull, player can challenge someone else

### 16. Drink Dominoes
**Description:** Play dominoes. When you can't place a domino, drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Deal 7 dominoes to each player
- Standard dominoes rules apply
- If you can't place a domino, drink and draw from boneyard
- First to use all dominoes gives drinks equal to sum of opponents' dominoes
- Play best of 3 rounds

### 17. Drink Darts
**Description:** Throw darts at a board. Miss the board and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player throws 3 darts per turn
- Miss the board completely = drink
- Score under 10 points = drink
- Highest score after each round gives out a drink
- After 3 rounds, increase distance to board

### 18. Drink Dice Poker
**Description:** Roll five dice. Lowest poker hand drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player rolls 5 dice
- One re-roll allowed for some or all dice
- Standard poker hand rankings apply (pair, two pair, three of a kind, etc.)
- Lowest hand drinks equal to difference between their hand and best hand
- After 3 rounds, winner creates a special rule

### 19. Drink Yahtzee
**Description:** Roll dice for Yahtzee combinations. Lowest score drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player gets 5 dice and up to 3 rolls
- Try to make Yahtzee combinations (full house, straight, etc.)
- Score according to Yahtzee rules
- Lowest score drinks equal to difference divided by 10
- After 3 rounds, winner creates a special rule

### 20. Drink Scattergories
**Description:** Write words starting with a specific letter in different categories. Duplicates drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Choose 5 categories and 1 letter
- 60 seconds to write one word per category
- Compare answers - duplicates with other players don't count
- Drink for each blank or duplicate answer
- Most unique answers chooses next letter

### 21. Drink Hangman
**Description:** Play Hangman. If the word is guessed, the word creator drinks. If not, the guessers drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player chooses a word or phrase
- Standard Hangman rules apply
- 6 wrong guesses allowed before "hanging"
- If word is guessed, creator drinks equal to remaining guesses
- If hangman is completed, all guessers drink

### 22. Drink Tic Tac Toe Tournament
**Description:** Play Tic Tac Toe. Losers drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Organize a tournament bracket
- Standard Tic Tac Toe rules apply
- Loser drinks and is eliminated
- Ties mean both players drink and replay
- Tournament winner gives out 5 drinks

### 23. Drink Connect Four
**Description:** Play Connect Four. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a Connect Four game or app
- Standard rules apply
- Loser drinks equal to number of connected pieces
- Winner challenges a new player
- After 3 consecutive wins, champion creates a rule

### 24. Drink Checkers
**Description:** Play a quick game of checkers. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a checkers board or app
- Play with 6 pieces each for quicker games
- Standard rules apply
- Loser drinks equal to pieces difference
- Winner challenges a new player

### 25. Drink Chess
**Description:** Play speed chess with a timer. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a chess board or app
- 30-second timer per move
- Exceed time limit = automatic drink
- Loser drinks equal to piece value difference
- Checkmate = finish your drink

### 26. Drink Backgammon
**Description:** Play a simplified version of backgammon. Loser drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Use a backgammon board or app
- Simplified rules for quicker play
- Doubles mean opponent drinks
- Getting hit sends you back and you drink
- Loser drinks equal to pieces not borne off

### 27. Drink Uno
**Description:** Play a round of Uno. Loser drinks based on cards left in hand.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Standard Uno rules apply
- Draw 2 = drink 2
- Skip = skip drinking round
- Reverse = reverse drinking order
- Wild = give out 2 drinks
- Loser drinks equal to points in their hand

## Hard Difficulty

### 1. Drink Kings Cup
**Description:** Play Kings Cup with complex rules and drinking penalties.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Place a cup in center surrounded by a circle of cards
- Each card rank has a specific rule:
  - Ace: Waterfall (everyone drinks, can't stop until person to right stops)
  - 2: You (choose someone to drink)
  - 3: Me (you drink)
  - 4: Floor (last to touch floor drinks)
  - 5: Guys (all men drink)
  - 6: Chicks (all women drink)
  - 7: Heaven (last to point up drinks)
  - 8: Mate (choose drinking buddy who drinks when you do)
  - 9: Rhyme (rhyming chain, fail = drink)
  - 10: Categories (category chain, fail = drink)
  - Jack: Rule (create a rule, breaking it = drink)
  - Queen: Question (answer a question = drink)
  - King: Pour (pour some drink into center cup)
- Person who draws 4th King drinks center cup

### 2. Drink Beer Pong Tournament
**Description:** Play a tournament-style beer pong with elimination rounds.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Organize teams of 2
- Standard beer pong rules with 6-10 cups
- Single elimination tournament
- Losing team drinks remaining cups
- Winners advance to next round
- Tournament champions create a rule for next game

### 3. Drink Flip Cup Championship
**Description:** Multi-round flip cup tournament with increasing difficulty.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Divide into equal teams
- Round 1: Standard flip cup
- Round 2: Non-dominant hand only
- Round 3: Eyes closed
- Losing team each round drinks penalty
- Championship team creates rule for next game

### 4. Drink Quarters Master
**Description:** Bounce quarters into increasingly smaller targets. Miss and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with large cup
- Each successful shot moves to smaller cup
- Miss and drink equal to current level
- Complete all levels to become Quarters Master
- Quarters Master assigns 10 drinks to distribute

### 5. Drink Trivia Gauntlet
**Description:** Answer progressively harder trivia questions. Wrong answers mean more drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- 5 rounds of increasing difficulty
- Round 1: 1 drink per wrong answer
- Round 2: 2 drinks per wrong answer
- And so on up to Round 5
- No help or devices allowed
- Last player standing is Trivia Master

### 6. Drink Shot Roulette
**Description:** Spin to determine who takes a shot from a selection of mixed drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Arrange 8-10 shots in circle
- 1-2 shots are stronger or different
- Spin bottle to determine who drinks
- No smelling or inspecting before drinking
- Must finish entire shot
- Continue until all shots are gone

### 7. Drink Power Hour
**Description:** Take a small drink every minute for an hour (simulated in-game).

**Additional Instructions:**
- Set timer for 1-minute intervals
- Everyone drinks at each interval
- Missing a drink = penalty of double
- Compressed version: 30 seconds for 30 minutes
- Last player remaining wins and assigns 5 drinks

### 8. Drink Stack Attack
**Description:** Stack cups in a specific pattern while timed. Fail and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with pyramid of 6 cups
- 30 seconds to unstack and restack in new pattern
- Fail or time out = drink equal to cups
- Each round adds more cups and complexity
- After 3 successful rounds, player can challenge someone else

### 9. Drink Speed Bartender
**Description:** Name as many cocktails as possible in 30 seconds. Lowest count drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Players take turns naming cocktails
- 30-second time limit per player
- No repeating cocktails already named
- Lowest count drinks equal to difference from highest
- Winner creates a special rule

### 10. Drink Pong Variations
**Description:** Beer pong with special rules like behind-the-back shots, non-dominant hand, etc.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Standard beer pong setup
- Each round has special rule:
  - Round 1: Non-dominant hand
  - Round 2: Behind the back
  - Round 3: Eyes closed
  - Round 4: Bounce shots only
  - Round 5: Trick shots only
- Losing team drinks all remaining cups

### 11. Drink Obstacle Course
**Description:** Complete a mini obstacle course while holding a drink. Spill and drink double.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Create simple obstacles (under table, over chair, etc.)
- Hold drink while completing course
- Time limit of 60 seconds
- Spilling = drink double
- Fastest time gives out 5 drinks

### 12. Drink Card House
**Description:** Build a house of cards. If it falls, drink based on how many cards were used.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player adds one card at a time
- If structure falls during your turn, drink equal to cards used
- First to successfully add 10 cards wins
- Winner assigns drinks equal to their cards
- Increase difficulty with unstable surfaces

### 13. Drink Dice Tower
**Description:** Stack dice on top of each other. If they fall, drink based on the sum.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Start with 1 die and add 1 each turn
- Stack vertically on smallest face (1 or pip side)
- If tower falls, drink equal to sum of dice
- First to stack 5 dice wins
- Winner assigns drinks equal to their stack height

### 14. Drink Blind Bartender
**Description:** Mix a cocktail blindfolded. Others rate it. Low scores drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Blindfold player and provide ingredients
- 2 minutes to mix a specified cocktail
- Other players rate taste on scale of 1-10
- Score below 5 = drink equal to 10 minus score
- Highest rated becomes next bartender

### 15. Drink Bottle Flip Challenge
**Description:** Flip bottles to land upright. Miss and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player gets 3 attempts per round
- Bottles must be partially filled
- Miss all 3 = drink equal to round number
- Land 2+ = assign a drink
- After 3 rounds, increase difficulty with smaller bottles

### 16. Drink Coin Stack
**Description:** Stack coins on edge. If they fall, drink based on how many were stacked.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player adds one coin at a time
- Coins must be stacked on edge
- If stack falls during your turn, drink equal to coins
- First to successfully add 7 coins wins
- Winner assigns drinks equal to their stack height

### 17. Drink Shot Ski
**Description:** Multiple players drink from attached shot glasses simultaneously. Last to finish drinks again.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Attach 3+ shot glasses to board/ruler
- Players must drink simultaneously
- Last to finish drinks an extra shot

### 18. Drink Chandelier
**Description:** Complex cup arrangement where landing a ball in the center means everyone drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Arrange cups in circle with center cup
- Outer cups = assign drinks

- Each player gets 2 attempts per turn

### 19. Drink Boat Race
**Description:** Teams race to finish a line of drinks. Losing team drinks an extra penalty.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Divide into equal teams
- Line up cups for each team
- Players drink sequentially (can't start until previous finishes)
- Losing team drinks penalty cup

**Description:** Fast-paced game with multiple cups, dice, and drinking penalties.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Arrange cups in circle with center cup
- Two players start with dice and cups

### 21. Drink Civil War
**Description:** Team-based beer pong with multiple cups and complex rules.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Two teams with 2+ players each
- Multiple balls in play simultaneously
- Redemption shots allowed
- Special cup rules (e.g., explosion cup, poison cup)

### 22. Drink Anchorman
**Description:** Last person to put their thumb on the table when the leader does drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- One player is the Anchorman
- Anchorman subtly puts thumb on table during conversation
- Others must notice and do the same
- Last person drinks
- New Anchorman is selected each round
- After 5 rounds, increase by requiring specific phrases

### 23. Drink Wizard Staff
**Description:** Stack empty cans/bottles to create a "staff". Shortest staff drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each finished drink adds to your "staff"
- Tape or stack empty containers
- Compare heights at designated times
- Shortest staff drinks penalty
- Falling staff means rebuild and drink
- Longest staff at end is Wizard and creates rule

### 24. Drink Centurion
**Description:** Take a shot of beer every minute for 100 minutes (simulated in-game).

**Additional Instructions:**
- Compressed version: 10 shots over 10 minutes
- Set timer for intervals
- Missing a shot = double next round
- No bathroom breaks without penalty
- Last player standing wins and assigns 5 drinks

### 25. Drink Battleshots
**Description:** Battleship game where hits mean drinks.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Create grid with shot glasses
- Place "ships" of 2-5 connected shots
- Take turns calling coordinates
- Hit = opponent drinks that shot
- Sink ship = finish your drink
- First to sink all ships wins

### 26. Drink Thumper
**Description:** Complex rhythm game with personalized gestures. Miss your cue and drink.

**Additional Instructions:**
- Each player creates personal gesture
- Leader sets rhythm by thumping table
- Leader does their gesture then another player's
- That player must respond with their gesture then another's
- Miss rhythm or gesture = drink
- After 3 mistakes, player creates new gesture
