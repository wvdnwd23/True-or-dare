# Drink Mode - Truth or Dare Deluxe

## Overview

Drink Mode is an adult-oriented extension of the Truth or Dare Deluxe app, designed for players aged 18+. It transforms the classic Truth or Dare game into a drinking game with structured rules, tracking features, and mini-games.

## Features

### Shot Counter

The Shot Counter keeps track of how many shots have been consumed during the game session:

- **Increment/Decrement**: Manually adjust the counter as shots are taken
- **Reset**: Clear the counter at the beginning of a new game
- **Visual Feedback**: Animated counter provides clear visual feedback

### Lie Cards

Each player starts with 3 Lie Cards that can be used strategically throughout the game:

- **Usage**: A player can use a Lie Card to avoid answering a Truth question truthfully
- **Limited Resource**: Once used, a Lie Card is gone for the remainder of the game
- **Penalty**: If caught lying without using a Lie Card, the player must take 4 shots

### Mini-Games

The app includes 20 different drinking mini-games of varying difficulty levels:

#### Easy Games
- **Never Have I Ever**: Players take turns saying "Never have I ever..." followed by something they've never done. Anyone who has done it takes a drink.
- **Most Likely To**: Someone asks "Who's most likely to...?" Everyone points at who they think is most likely. Person with most points drinks that many shots.
- **Waterfall**: Everyone starts drinking at the same time. No one can stop until the person to their right stops.
- **Thumb Master**: Designated player can put their thumb on the table anytime. Last player to notice and put their thumb down drinks.
- **Medusa**: Everyone looks down. On count of 3, look up at someone. If they're looking at you too, both drink.
- **Coin Flip**: Predict heads or tails. If wrong, drink. If right, give out a drink.
- **Staring Contest**: Two players stare at each other. First to blink or laugh drinks.
- **Rock Paper Scissors**: Loser of each round drinks. Play best of 3 or 5.

#### Medium Games
- **Categories**: Choose a category (e.g., car brands). Go around the circle naming items in that category. First to hesitate or repeat takes a shot.
- **Word Association**: First player says a word, next player must say a related word. Continue until someone hesitates or repeats a word.
- **Quarters**: Bounce a quarter into a shot glass. If you make it, choose someone to drink.
- **Straight Face**: Players take turns trying to make others laugh while they try to keep a straight face. First to laugh drinks.
- **Rhyme Time**: First player says a word, next player must say a word that rhymes. Continue until someone can't think of a rhyme.
- **Alphabet Game**: Choose a category. First player says something in that category starting with A, next with B, and so on.

#### Hard Games
- **Flip Cup**: Divide into two teams. Each player drinks and then tries to flip their cup upside down. First team to finish wins.
- **Beer Pong**: Set up cups in a triangle at each end of a table. Take turns throwing ping pong balls into opponent's cups. If it lands, they drink.
- **Kings Cup**: Place a cup in the center surrounded by a circle of cards. Take turns drawing cards, each with a different rule.
- **Ride the Bus**: A multi-round card game where players guess card values. Wrong guesses mean drinks.
- **Buzz**: Count around the circle, replacing any number containing 7 or divisible by 7 with 'buzz'. Mess up and drink.
- **Chandelier**: Center cup surrounded by outer cups. Land ball in center, everyone drinks. Land in outer cup, assign that drink.

### Drinking Rules

The app enforces a set of consistent drinking rules:

1. **Truth Penalty**: If you refuse to answer a Truth, take 2 shots.
2. **Dare Penalty**: If you refuse to perform a Dare, take 3 shots.
3. **Lie Card**: Each player starts with 3 Lie Cards. Use one to avoid answering truthfully.
4. **Caught Lying**: If caught lying without using a Lie Card, take 4 shots.
5. **Mini-Game Loser**: The loser of a mini-game takes 1 shot.
6. **Phone Check**: If you check your phone during the game, take 1 shot.
7. **Name Slip**: If you say someone's name instead of 'the person to my left/right', take 1 shot.
8. **Bathroom Break**: Take 1 shot if you need a bathroom break (except for emergencies).
9. **Laugh Control**: If you laugh during a serious truth question, take 1 shot.
10. **Drink Spill**: If you spill your drink, take 2 shots as penalty.

## Responsible Drinking

The Truth or Dare Deluxe app promotes responsible drinking:

- **Age Verification**: Drink Mode is only available to users 18+
- **Moderation Reminders**: The app includes periodic reminders to drink water
- **Designated Driver**: Option to mark a player as designated driver (no alcohol)
- **Emergency Contacts**: Quick access to emergency contacts and ride services
- **Session Limits**: Option to set time or drink limits for game sessions

## Customization

Drink Mode can be customized to suit different preferences:

- **Rule Adjustments**: Enable/disable specific rules
- **Shot Size**: Adjust what counts as a "shot" (standard, half, or double)
- **Difficulty**: Adjust the overall difficulty of mini-games
- **Custom Rules**: Add your own custom drinking rules

## Accessing Drink Mode

Drink Mode can be accessed from the main player selection screen or enabled in the settings menu.

## Disclaimer

Truth or Dare Deluxe encourages responsible drinking. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive.
