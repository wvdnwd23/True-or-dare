# Truth or Dare - Category & Sub-Option Descriptions

This document provides concise descriptions for all categories and sub-options to be used in the UI as tooltips and explanatory text.

## Categories

### Soft
**Description:** Light-hearted, fun questions and dares suitable for all ages and settings. Perfect for breaking the ice and getting everyone comfortable.

**Tooltip:** Family-friendly fun that everyone can enjoy. Great for starting the game!

**Sub-Options:**
- **Icebreaker:** Perfect for starting the game and getting everyone comfortable. Simple questions and easy dares to warm up the group.
- **Silly:** Playful and humorous questions and dares that bring out the laughter. Expect giggles and light-hearted moments.
- **Creative:** Challenges that spark imagination and creative thinking. Show off your inventive side with these prompts.

### Medium
**Description:** Moderately challenging questions and dares with a bit more spice. Pushes boundaries slightly while keeping things appropriate for most settings.

**Tooltip:** A step up in challenge level. Slightly more daring but still appropriate for most groups.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Challenging:** Questions and dares that push you slightly out of your comfort zone. Nothing extreme, but expect to reveal more than with Soft prompts.
- **Entertaining:** Fun activities that entertain the whole group. These prompts are designed to create memorable moments and laughs.
- **Surprising:** Unexpected questions and dares that keep the game interesting. Be prepared for the unpredictable!

### Hot (18+)
**Description:** Spicy questions and dares for adult players looking for excitement. Explores attraction, desire, and romantic scenarios.

**Tooltip:** 18+ content with flirtatious and sensual themes. For adult players only.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Flirty:** Playful flirtation and light romantic challenges. Explores attraction and chemistry in a fun way.
- **Sensual:** Questions and dares focused on sensuality and attraction. More suggestive than Flirty, but still tasteful.
- **Daring:** Bold challenges that test your courage in romantic contexts. For those willing to take romantic risks.

### Extreme (18+)
**Description:** The most intense questions and dares for adventurous adult players. Pushes boundaries and explores taboo topics.

**Tooltip:** Our most intense 18+ content. For adventurous adults comfortable with explicit themes.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Wild:** Outrageous challenges that push boundaries. Expect the unexpected and be ready to step far outside your comfort zone.
- **Risqué:** Provocative questions and dares for the bold. Explores intimate topics and scenarios without holding back.
- **Uninhibited:** For those who want to leave all inhibitions behind. Our most boundary-pushing content for the truly adventurous.

### Couples
**Description:** Questions and dares designed to bring couples closer together. Focuses on relationship dynamics, communication, and fun.

**Tooltip:** Strengthen your bond and have fun together with prompts designed specifically for couples.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Bonding:** Activities that strengthen your connection. Focuses on communication, understanding, and emotional intimacy.
- **Romantic:** Sweet and loving challenges to enhance your relationship. Rekindle the romance with these heartfelt prompts.
- **Playful:** Fun and lighthearted activities for couples to enjoy together. Add some laughter and joy to your relationship.

### Couples Hot (18+)
**Description:** Intimate questions and dares for adult couples seeking to spice things up. Explores physical connection and desire.

**Tooltip:** 18+ content for couples looking to deepen intimacy and explore desire together.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Intimate:** Deep connection and closeness for committed partners. Focuses on emotional and physical intimacy.
- **Passionate:** Fiery challenges to ignite the passion in your relationship. Rekindle the flame with these steamy prompts.
- **Adventurous:** Exciting new experiences to explore together. Push boundaries and discover new aspects of your relationship.

### Dirty (18+)
**Description:** Explicitly suggestive questions and dares for adult players. Our most suggestive category that explores fantasies and desires.

**Tooltip:** Explicit 18+ content with suggestive themes and language. For adults comfortable with direct sexual content.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Suggestive:** Hints and innuendos that leave little to the imagination. Playfully provocative without being explicit.
- **Explicit:** Direct and unambiguous adult content. Our most straightforward sexual content for those who prefer no filters.
- **Fantasy:** Exploration of desires and fantasies. Dive into imagination and discover new possibilities.

### Deep Past
**Description:** Questions that explore your history, memories, and formative experiences. Reflect on your journey and share meaningful stories.

**Tooltip:** Reflect on your past experiences and share the moments that shaped who you are today.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Memories:** Recalling significant moments from your past. Revisit the experiences that stand out in your memory.
- **Reflections:** Looking back on how past events shaped who you are today. Connect the dots between your experiences and your current self.
- **Lessons:** Exploring what you've learned from past experiences. Share the wisdom you've gained through life's journey.

### Mental Health (Opt-In)
**Description:** Thoughtful questions about emotional wellbeing and mental health. Creates space for meaningful conversations about feelings and self-care.

**Tooltip:** Supportive prompts about emotional wellbeing. Requires opt-in as topics may be sensitive for some players.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Awareness:** Understanding your own mental health patterns. Explore how you experience and process emotions.
- **Coping:** Strategies and approaches for managing challenges. Share what helps you navigate difficult feelings and situations.
- **Growth:** Moving forward and developing emotional resilience. Focus on how you've evolved and strengthened your mental wellbeing.

### Childhood
**Description:** Questions about your early years and formative experiences. Revisit the wonder, challenges, and simplicity of childhood.

**Tooltip:** Nostalgic prompts about growing up and early experiences that shaped you.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Nostalgia:** Fond memories from your younger years. Revisit the joyful, carefree moments of childhood.
- **Experiences:** Significant events that shaped your childhood. Explore the defining moments of your early years.
- **Influences:** People and factors that impacted your early development. Reflect on who and what helped shape your foundation.

### Self-Growth
**Description:** Questions focused on personal development and improvement. Explore your journey of becoming your best self.

**Tooltip:** Explore your personal development journey and aspirations for becoming your best self.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Goals:** Aspirations and targets for your personal journey. Share what you're working toward and hoping to achieve.
- **Challenges:** Obstacles you've overcome or are working to overcome. Discuss the difficulties that have tested and strengthened you.
- **Insights:** Realizations and understandings about yourself. Share the "aha moments" that have changed your perspective.

### Trauma & Healing (Explicit Opt-In)
**Description:** Sensitive questions about difficult experiences and recovery. Creates space for sharing journeys of resilience and healing.

**Tooltip:** Supportive prompts about overcoming difficult experiences. Requires explicit opt-in due to potentially triggering content.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Processing:** Working through difficult experiences. Explore how you've made sense of challenging events in your life.
- **Recovery:** Steps taken toward healing and wellness. Share the practices and approaches that have helped you heal.
- **Resilience:** Building strength and moving forward after hardship. Focus on how you've grown stronger through adversity.

### Personal
**Description:** Questions about your individual traits, preferences, and characteristics. Discover what makes you uniquely you.

**Tooltip:** Explore what makes you uniquely you—your preferences, quirks, and defining characteristics.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Values:** Core beliefs and principles that guide your life. Explore what matters most to you and why.
- **Preferences:** Your likes, dislikes, and personal tastes. Share what you're drawn to and what you avoid.
- **Quirks:** Unique traits and characteristics that make you who you are. Celebrate the distinctive aspects of your personality.

### Self-Discovery
**Description:** Questions that help you learn more about yourself and your identity. Explore who you are at your core and what drives you.

**Tooltip:** Dive deeper into understanding yourself, your purpose, and what makes you authentic.

**Sub-Options:**
- **Identity:** Understanding who you are at your core. Explore the fundamental aspects of how you see yourself.
- **Purpose:** Exploring your meaning and direction in life. Reflect on what gives your life significance and where you're headed.
- **Authenticity:** Being true to yourself and your genuine nature. Discuss how you express your true self and overcome pressures to conform.
